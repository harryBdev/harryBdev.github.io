Research

I am completing a PhD in Accounting/Auditing at the Athens University of Economics and Business (AUEB). My work examines Key Audit Matters (KAMs), the entity-specific risks that auditors report in the expanded audit report, and what they reveal about audit practice and financial reporting.

Research interests

  • Key Audit Matters
  • Audit report communication
  • Audit quality and financial reporting quality
  • Fraud
  • Information security
  • Technology risk in governance
